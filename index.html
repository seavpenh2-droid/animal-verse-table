Animal Table

Mammals

Tiger

Tigers are large wild cats known for their orange fur and black stripes. They are powerful hunters and are mainly found in Asia.

Lion

Lions are large cats that live mainly in Africa. They are famous for living together in groups called prides.

Elephant

Elephants are the largest land animals on Earth. They are known for their trunks, large ears, strong memories, and social behavior.

Giraffe

Giraffes are tall African mammals famous for their extremely long necks and legs. They use their height to reach leaves high in trees.

Bear

Bears are powerful mammals found in many parts of the world. Different species live in forests, mountains, Arctic regions, and other habitats.

Wolf

Wolves are intelligent social predators that often live and hunt in family groups called packs. They are closely related to domestic dogs.




Birds

Owl

Owls are birds of prey known for their large eyes, excellent hearing, and ability to hunt at night. They can fly very quietly to surprise their prey.

Parrot

Parrots are colorful and intelligent birds. Many species can learn sounds and imitate human speech.

Eagle

Eagles are powerful birds of prey with strong claws and excellent eyesight. They use their sharp vision to locate prey from far away.

Peacock

Peacocks are famous for their beautiful, colorful tail feathers. Males spread their large tails during courtship displays.

Flamingo

Flamingos are tall birds with long legs, long necks, and curved bills. Their pink color comes mainly from the food they eat.




Reptiles

Chameleon

Chameleons are reptiles famous for their independently moving eyes, long sticky tongues, and ability to change their skin colors.

Crocodile

Crocodiles are large aquatic reptiles with powerful jaws and strong tails. They are ancient predators that have existed for millions of years.

Turtle

Turtles are reptiles protected by hard shells. Depending on the species, they can live in oceans, rivers, lakes, or on land.

Snake

Snakes are legless reptiles with long flexible bodies. They use their specialized senses to locate prey and survive in many different habitats.

Komodo Dragon

The Komodo dragon is the world's largest living lizard. It is a powerful predator native to several Indonesian islands.




Aquatic

Great White Shark

Great white sharks are powerful ocean predators known for their size, sharp teeth, and excellent senses. They live in coastal and offshore waters around the world.

Dolphin

Dolphins are highly intelligent marine mammals known for their social behavior, communication, and ability to use echolocation to navigate and find prey.

Octopus

Octopuses are intelligent marine animals with eight arms. They can change their color and texture to communicate, hide, or surprise prey.

Squid

Squid are fast-moving marine animals with streamlined bodies, tentacles, and large eyes. Some species live deep in the ocean.

Blue Whale

Blue whales are the largest animals known to have ever lived on Earth. Despite their enormous size, they mainly feed on tiny shrimp-like animals called krill.

Penguin

Penguins are flightless birds adapted for life in the water. Their wings have evolved into flippers that help them swim efficiently.

Seal

Seals are marine mammals with streamlined bodies and flippers. They spend much of their lives in the water but often come onto land or ice to rest and raise their young.

Humpback Whale

Humpback whales are large baleen whales famous for their long pectoral fins, powerful jumps, and complex songs.

Sperm Whale

Sperm whales are the largest toothed whales. They are famous for their enormous heads and ability to dive deep into the ocean to hunt squid.

Crab

Crabs are crustaceans with hard protective shells and powerful claws. They live in oceans, rivers, beaches, mangroves, and many other environments.




Insects

Ant

Ants are small social insects that live in organized colonies. They work together to find food, protect their nest, and care for their young.

Honey Bee

Honey bees are important insects that collect nectar and pollen from flowers. They help plants reproduce through pollination and produce honey.

Butterfly

Butterflies are colorful insects with delicate wings. They begin life as caterpillars and later transform into adults through metamorphosis.

Beetle

Beetles are insects with hard outer wing coverings that protect their bodies. They are one of the most diverse groups of animals on Earth.
